Global economy and climate action

Over the last 50 years, the global economy has grown to almost 5x its size.

- This is Due in large part to the tripling in extraction of natural resources and energy that has fueled the growth in
  production and consumption
- Population has doubled to 7.8 billion
    - Prosperity has also doubled on average, but about 1.3 billion people remain poor and around 700 million are
      hungry.

Climate finance is financing that seeks to support mitigation and adaptation actions that will address climate change

- In 2019-2020 the global annual climate finance average was $803 billion
- Developing countries will require $6 trillion in climate finance to repair the climate by 2030
