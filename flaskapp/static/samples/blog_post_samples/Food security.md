Food security

Food security is when all people, at all times, have physical and economic access to sufficient safe and nutritious food
that meets their dietary needs and food preferences for an active and healthy life.

- There are 4 main dimensions of food security:
    - Physical availability of food
        - This addresses the "supply side" and is determined by the level of food production, stock levels and net trade
    - Economic and physical access to food
        - a household's ability to acquire an adequate amount of food regularly through a combination of purchases,
          barter,
          borrowings, food assistance of gifts
    - Food utilisation
        - Sufficient energy and nutrient intake by individuals are the result of good care and feeding practices, food
          preparation, diversity of the diet amd intra-household distribution of food
    - Stability of the other 3 dimensions over time
        - Adverse weather conditions, political instability, or economic factors (unemployment, rising food prices) may
          have
          an impact on your food security status.

Food Security is threatened by the loss of pollinators and fertile soil. Loss of pollinators threatens annual global
crop
output worth between $235 billion and $577 billion
