Human Impact

3/4 of the worlds land and 2/3 of its oceans are now impacted by humans.

1 million of the world's estimated 8 million species of flora and fauna are threatened wih extinction.

Many ecosystem services essential for human well-being are eroding.

In just 2018, damages from climate-related natural disasters cost about $155 billion.

Land degradation negatively impacts more than 3 billion people.
