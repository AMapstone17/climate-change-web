The temperature of the world

- At it's current rate the world's temperature will exceed 1.5°C by 2035, and is projected to increase to 2.5°C by 2100
    - To counter this, we would need a green house gas reduction of 43% by 2030, and to reach net-zero by 2050
- A significant factor influencing that increase is humans. Human-induced current warming of more than 1°C has already
  led to:
    - shifts in climate zones
    - changes in precipitation patterns
    - melting of ice sheets and glaciers
    - accelerating sea level rise
    - more frequent and more intense extreme events, threatening people and nature
- The current negative environmental changes being caused by human activity are impeding:
    - Progress towards ending poverty and hunger
    - reducing inequalities
    - promoting sustainable economic growth
    - work for all
    - peaceful and inclusive societies
