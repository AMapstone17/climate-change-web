The 5 targets of climate action

### The sustainable development goal of Climate Action consists of 5 main targets to be achieved:

- Strengthen resilience and adaptive capacity to climate related disasters in all countries
- Build knowledge and capacity to meet climate change
    - This involves improving education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change
      mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning.
- Promote mechanisms to raise capacity for planning and management
    - This involves promoting mechanisms for raising capacity for effective climate change-related planning and
      management
      in least developed countries and small island developing states, including focussing on women, youth and local and
      marginalised communities.
- Integrate c;imate change measures into national policies, strategies and planning
- Implement the UN framework convention on climate change
    - This involves implementing the commitment undertaken by the developed-country parties to the united nations
      framework convention on climate change to a goal of mobilising jointly $100 billion annually by 2020 from all
      sources to address the needs of developing countries in the context of meaningful mitigation actions and
      transparency on implementation and fully operationalise the green climate fund through its capitalisation as soon
      as
      possible.
