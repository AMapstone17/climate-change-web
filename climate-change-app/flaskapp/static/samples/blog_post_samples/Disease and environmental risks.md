Disease and environmental risks

Around 1/4 of the global burden of disease stems from environment-related risks, including:

- Animal-borne diseases
- Climate change
- Exposure to pollution and toxic chemicals

Pollution causes more than 9 million premature deaths annually and millions more die every year from the other
environment-related health risks.
